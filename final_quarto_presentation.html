Uber Ride Analytics in NCR India

Agenda

  • Policy use case & motivation

  • Data source & scope

  • Findings from visualizations

  • Interactive demo

  • Key takeaways & next steps

Policy use case

Ride-hailing reliability and pricing transparency affect:

  • Two focus areas:

    • Driver reliability and platform efficiency

    • Fairness perceptions (especially for short urban trips)

Data source

  • Dataset: Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard dataset (Kaggle)

  • Geography: National Capital Region(NCR), India

  • Unit of analysis: individual bookings

  • Key fields used: Booking Status, Booking Value, Ride Distance, Time, Vehicle Type

Visualization 1: Price per Kilometer by Trip Distance

Interpretation: Price vs Distance

  • Fares generally increase with distance, but not strictly linearly

  • Short trips show greater pricing dispersion

  • Distance alone does not explain what riders pay, especially for short urban trips

  • Perhaps base fare minimums / surges matter most for short urban rides

Visualization 2: Cancellation Rate by hour of day

Cancellation Rates Interpretation

  • Cancellation rates are stable overall (within ~5 percentage points)
  • Highest point occurs early morning (low supply effect)
  • Commute windows have an average rate

Visualization 3: Peak Usage Hours

Interpretation: peak usage hours for auto

  • Demand follows a predictable daily cycle
  • Two peaks: late morning and stronger evening peak
  • Predictable timing enables proactive driver supply planning

Visualization 4: Peak-hour comparison (weekday vs weekend)

Interpretation: Weekday vs Weekend Demand

  • Peak-hour patterns are similar between weekdays and weekends

  • Suggests operational levers should be calendar-aware

  • Supports targeted policies rather than one-size-fits-all

Visualization 5: High-Traffic Routes by Cancellation Rate

Interpretation: Route-Level Risk

  • High cancellation risk is corridor-specific
  • High volume does not guarantee reliability
  • Supports route-specific interventions
  • Justifies interactive drill-down

Visualization 6: Shiny Interactive Tool

Demo goal: show how anyone can explore:

  • Differences in Vehicle Type

  • Cancellations by Driver or Customer only

  • Filter by minimum Cancellation rate

Key takeaways

  • Pricing efficiency: short rides are costlier per km and less predictable

  • Reliability: cancellations fluctuate modestly but follow predictable timing

  • Decision support: interactive tools enable fast drill-down for operations

Next steps

  • Model cancellation probability using hour + distance + other features

  • Add geography (pickup/drop) to locate repeated problem corridors

  • Improve measurement of surge / dynamic pricing if available
